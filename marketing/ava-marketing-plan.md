## 1. Positioning + Core Message

**The wedge:** A small DTC brand owner has *product photos* but no *product video*. Every AI video tool they've tried either (a) is too technical, or (b) mangles their product — wrong logo, melting label, six fingers on the model holding the necklace. AVA sells the outcome, not the tool: a clean, on-brand product video, done for them, for the price of a nice dinner.

### Headline (primary)
**"Your product deserves a video. Not a science project."**
*Subhead:* We turn one product photo into a scroll-stopping AI video — and we audit every clip so your logo, label, and colors survive. From $59. No subscription, no software to learn.

**Alternates (copywriting skill — 2-3 options):**
- **A.** "We audited the 105 ways AI video fails — so yours won't." *(authority/differentiator lead — best for Creators Wall + cold email)*
- **B.** "One photo in. One video your customers stop scrolling for, out." *(outcome lead — best for IG/TikTok bio + DTC owners)*
- **C.** "Product videos for brands that can't afford a film crew." *(price/access lead — best for RED / cost-conscious makers)*

### Three proof points
1. **Per-clip product-consistency QC.** Generic tools hand you whatever the model dreamed up. We check every clip against your actual product — the differentiator no competitor names out loud.
2. **The Creators Wall.** 64 real AI videos with the *exact prompts* that made them (Sora, Runway Gen-3, plus AVA Studio samples). Proof we know the craft, and a public library nobody else publishes.
3. **Flat, one-time price.** $59 / $149 / $229. No $30/mo tool you forget to cancel. You pay once, you get the video, you post it.

**Voice:** Practitioner who has actually shipped AI video — dry, specific, a little battle-scarred. Never "revolutionary AI-powered synergy." Uses the customer's words ("the label came out wrong," "it looks like a video," "will it match my brand").

## 4. Free-Sample Outbound Playbook (the revenue engine)

**Thesis:** Talking is cheap; a finished video is not. We do the expensive thing *first, free, once* — then ask for the sale on the strength of the result.

### Step 1 — Source targets (DTC brands with weak/no video)
**Where to find them (free, repeatable):**
- **Instagram hashtag mining:** `#handmadejewelry #smallbusinesscheck #candlebusiness #skincareroutine #dogtreats #hotsauce #shopsmall #etsyseller`. Filter for accounts with **good product photos but zero/weak Reels** — that gap is the whole pitch.
- **Etsy → IG:** Etsy shops almost always link an IG. Etsy "handmade" + "new/rising" filters surface exactly the under-resourced makers who can't afford a crew.
- **TikTok Shop / small sellers** posting slideshow-only content (photo carousels set to music) — they *want* video and don't have it.
- **小紅書:** search 手作 / 小众品牌 / 独立设计 sellers posting static product 图.
- **Qualifier (the filter):** <20k followers, sells a *physical* product, has ≥3 clean product photos, and either no Reels or only static slideshows. Skip anyone already running polished video (not our buyer).

**Sourcing target:** build a rolling list of **10 qualified brands/day** (name, IG handle, product, the specific gap you noticed, one usable product photo). Log in a simple sheet: `handle | product | gap | photo saved? | DM sent | reply | sample made | paid`.

### Step 2 — The free-sample offer
Pick the 3-5 best targets/day. Make **one** short AI product video from a photo already on their public feed (no asking permission to start — it's a gift, not a contract). Keep it to a 5-8s clip that visibly keeps their product consistent. This *is* the marketing asset and the sales pitch in one.

### Step 3 — The DM (copywriting skill — practitioner, not vendor)

**Ready-to-send DM template (cold, first touch):**
> Hey [name] — your [product, e.g. "hammered brass hoops"] photos are genuinely nice. I make AI product videos and got curious what yours would look like moving, so I made you a quick one from the shot on your feed — no charge, it's yours to post: [link/attach].
>
> If you like it I do these properly (any product, your call on style) — but honestly just wanted you to have it. Want me to send the full-res?

Why it works: leads with *their* world (their photos), the gift is already made (reciprocity), zero pressure, one low-friction ask ("want the full-res?"). No price, no feature list, no "revolutionary AI."

**Variant for RED / Chinese-speaking sellers:**
> 你好！看到你家[产品]的图拍得很好看，我做AI产品视频，顺手用你主页的一张图做了个短视频，送你，可以直接发~ 如果喜欢我可以做完整版（任意产品、风格你定）。要不要我把高清版发你？

### Step 4 — Convert to paid
When they reply positively:
> Glad you like it. Full-res is ready — it's $59 for the one, or if you want a set for a launch/season it's 3 for $149 (works out cheaper per video). Which fits? I'll send the order link.

Keep it one message, one decision. Send the Stripe link *after* they pick. If they go quiet after the sample: one soft nudge in 48h ("still want the full-res? no rush"), then drop it — the free clip already earned goodwill and a possible referral.

**Volume math for the month:** 30-40 DMs/day → ~15-20 free samples/wk → at a conservative 3-5% sample→paid, ~2-4 orders in 30 days. First paying order is very achievable; the sample quality is the lever.

## 5. Cold-Email Sequence (DTC brands)

For brands with a real website/email (Shopify DTC), email complements DMs. Plain-text, one ask, no images/HTML. **3 touches.** (cold-email skill.)

**Prospecting:** Shopify DTC brands in jewelry/beauty/food/pet with photo-only PDPs (product detail pages) and no product video. Find via IG bio links, Shopify store directories, or brands running static image ads.

### Touch 1 — Day 0
**Subject:** product video
> Hi [name],
>
> Your [product] page has great photos but no video — most [category] brands are in the same spot because video used to mean hiring a crew.
>
> I make AI product videos from a single photo (I QC each one so your label/logo doesn't warp — that's usually where AI falls apart). Happy to make you one free from a photo on your site so you can see it on your own product, not a demo.
>
> Want me to send one over?
>
> — [Name], aivideoauditor.com

### Touch 2 — Day 3 (new angle: proof)
**Subject:** re: product video
> Hi [name] — quick follow-up. Here's the kind of thing I mean: [link to one before→after on the Creators Wall / a sample].
>
> Static photo in, that video out, ~$59. No subscription — you pay once and post it. Still happy to make yours free first. Worth a look?

### Touch 3 — Day 7 (breakup)
**Subject:** last one
> Hi [name] — I'll stop here so I'm not cluttering your inbox. If product video ever moves up the list, the free-sample offer stands — just reply "video" and I'll make one from a photo on your site. Either way, your product photography is genuinely good.
>
> — [Name]

**Rules honored:** peer voice, "you/your" dominates, one CTA per email, each touch adds something new (offer → proof → graceful exit), boring internal-looking subjects. Cap: 25-40 new prospects/day; never blast identical bodies — swap the category/product observation per recipient.

## 6. SEO Angle — the Creators Wall compounds

**Why it works:** The Wall already holds **64 real AI videos with their exact prompts** (16 Sora, 44 Runway Gen-3, 4 AVA Studio; tags: cinematic, people, urban, aerial, nature, macro, product, food). "AI video prompt" search demand is large and growing, poorly served by structured, *citable* pages. This is a searchable **and** shareable asset — exactly what compounds (content-strategy skill), and it earns AI citations (ai-seo skill).

**How it compounds:**
- Each video → an extractable page: title, model, the full prompt, a 40-60 word "why this prompt works" block, tags. That structure is what ChatGPT/Perplexity cite and what Google AI Overviews summarize.
- Hub/spoke: a **hub** page "AI Video Prompts (Sora & Runway)" → **spokes** per model and per shot-type (product, macro, food). Interlink.
- Add `FAQPage` + `Article`/`VideoObject` schema, an `llms.txt`, and keep AI crawlers (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended) allowed in robots.txt so the Wall can actually be cited.
- Authority levers (Princeton GEO): the prompts *are* the data/specifics; add "last updated" dates; attribute the QC/craft notes. Cite the model docs.
- **The funnel:** prompt searcher → Wall page → sees AVA Studio samples alongside Sora/Runway → "or skip the 40 failed generations, we'll make it for $59" → Order.

### 10 target keywords / topics
1. AI video prompts (hub)
2. Sora prompts / best Sora video prompts
3. Runway Gen-3 prompts
4. AI product video (commercial intent — high value)
5. how to make an AI video from a photo
6. AI video for small business / Etsy / handmade
7. cinematic AI video prompt formula
8. why does AI video look fake / AI video mistakes (pain, ties to 105-failures)
9. AI video for jewelry / candles / skincare / pet products (per-vertical spokes)

## 8. DAILY EXECUTION CHECKLIST

> Run every day. ~2-2.5 hrs. Batch content on Mondays (see weekly). Respect: 1 primary post/platform/day, no link-drop in comments, human/practitioner voice, vary wording (anti-spam).

### Every day (Mon-Fri core; lighter Sat/Sun)
- [ ] **Source 10 qualified targets** (IG hashtags / Etsy→IG / TikTok slideshow sellers / RED 手作). Log to sheet with the specific gap + save one product photo each.
- [ ] **Make 3-5 free-sample clips** from sourced photos (5-8s, product-consistency checked).
- [ ] **Send 30-40 DMs** across IG + TikTok + RED (each with its made sample). Vary the opener per recipient.
- [ ] **Send 25-40 cold emails** (Touch 1 to new prospects; Touch 2/3 to the sequence queue). Plain text, one ask.
- [ ] **Post 1 primary piece per platform:** 1 IG Reel + 1 TikTok + (RED on its cadence days). Pull from the batched queue.
- [ ] **Post 3-5 IG Stories** (behind-the-studio, poll, "made this today").
- [ ] **Engage 8-10 comments** on target/maker accounts — substantive, address their post first, zero links.
- [ ] **Reply to 100% of DMs/comments/email replies.** Move positive replies → convert-to-paid message + Stripe link. Log outcomes.
- [ ] **Log the day's numbers** (targets, DMs, samples, emails, posts, replies) in the tracking sheet.

### Weekly rhythm (layer onto daily)
- **Mon — Batch + Plan:** write/queue the week's 5 Reels + 5-7 TikToks + 3-4 RED 笔记 (2-3 hrs). Pick the week's "Prompt of the Week" from the Wall. Publish 1-2 Creators-Wall SEO pages.
- **Tue:** Before→After showcase day (P1). Send Touch-2 follow-ups.
- **Wed:** "How I made this" / prompt teardown (P2) → repurpose to all 4 formats. Publish 1 more Wall SEO page.
- **Thu:** Pain-hook day (P3). Send Touch-3 breakups; clear the email queue.
- **Fri:** Behind-the-studio / results (P4). **Weekly review:** top 3 / bottom 3 posts, reply rate, sample→paid rate, best posting time. Adjust next week's targets.
- **Sat/Sun (light):** engagement only (comments + DM replies), source targets for Monday, no new outbound blast.

### Convert-to-paid micro-loop (whenever a positive reply lands)
1. Confirm they like the sample.
2. One message: full-res = $59 / set = 3 for $149 — which fits?
3. On pick → send Stripe link. On silence → one 48h nudge, then drop (keep goodwill).
